A Message From My Heart

Dear Sachin, ❤️

Today is your day, but honestly, I feel like the lucky one. Having you as my brother is one of the greatest blessings of my life, and I don't think I say that often enough.

You mean more to me than I can put into words. In every phase of my life, good or hard, just knowing you are there makes me feel stronger and less alone. You are not only my brother, you are my friend, my support and a part of who I am.

Our bond is something I will always treasure. From the smallest everyday moments to the unforgettable memories we have created together, every moment holds a special place in my heart. Even our silly fights, random jokes, endless laughter, and those little moments that only we understand are memories I would never trade for anything.

I want to see you succeed in everything you dream of. I want you to stay happy, healthy and smiling, and I will always be right behind you, cheering the loudest, whatever you choose to do.

No matter how much time passes or how far life takes us, our brotherhood will always stay special. Thank you for being you, Sachin .

Always yours,
Aashu
